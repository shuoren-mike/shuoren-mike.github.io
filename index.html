Shuo Ren

PhD Candidate · Computer Science and Engineering · The Chinese University of Hong Kong

I am currently a third-year PhD candidate in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), affiliated with IDEALab. I am supervised by Prof. Tsung-Yi Ho (IEEE Fellow) and work closely with Prof. Bei Yu. I am also a research intern at Hong Kong Science Park.

My research lies in Electronic Design Automation (EDA), with a focus on 3D integrated circuits, chiplet-based design, and algorithmic optimization for physical design. More recently I have been building long-horizon, tool-grounded LLM agents for complex engineering workflows such as physical-design QoR optimization and post-route ECO.

Selected Research

ASP-DAC 2027

AgenticPD: Stage-Aware Agentic Physical Design QoR Optimization

Formulates physical-design QoR tuning as a stage-aware tree search over floorplan, placement, CTS and routing checkpoints, with a Judge Agent navigating reusable intermediate design states.

Paper Code
Preprint 2026

AgenticECO: Failure-Conditioned Agentic Repair for Post-Route ECO

Formulates post-route ECO for 3D-IC design as failure-conditioned agentic repair. Typed timing, routing, layout and violation evidence drives deterministic repair programs executed outside the LLM, and every edit must pass immutable DRC, timing, equivalence and preservation gates.

Paper Code
ASP-DAC 2026

Partitioning-free 3D-IC Floorplan

Removes the up-front netlist partitioning step and jointly optimizes block placement and die assignment directly under physical constraints.

Paper Slides
Preprint 2026

CLIP-3D: Architecture-level Shift-left in 3D-IC Design

Connects gem5, McPAT, CACTI, HotSpot and analytical 3D floorplanning to expose floorplan-driven thermal, wire and cache effects during architecture exploration. Improves realized BIPS by 13.75% on average across thermally binding configurations.

Paper
Preprint 2026

Chiplet3D: Pin- and Thermal-Aware 3D Chiplet Floorplanning

Embeds exact pin transformations and a convolutional 3D thermal field inside a MILP floorplanner to co-optimize wirelength and temperature. Cuts wirelength by 39–43% and peak temperature by up to 45.9 °C versus prior baselines.

Paper
Preprint 2026

A²utoLPBench: Auto-Generated LP Benchmark via Inverse-KKT

Replaces fixed LP-from-text corpora with an inverse-KKT generator and an agent-runnable Docker environment that produces unlimited problems with certified optima and tunable difficulty.

Paper
Under submission

3D-IC Rectilinear Floorplanning

Unifies global rectilinear floorplanning and legalization for 3D-IC layouts with irregular block shapes.
